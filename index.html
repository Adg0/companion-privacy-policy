Temperance Companion

Privacy Policy

Temperance Companion reads fitness and health metrics from Health Connect only after you grant access. It never writes to Health Connect.

An explicit refresh reads available records from all Health Connect apps for the past seven local days based on your configuration. Export writes the reviewed preview and local habit sessions as one staging file you specified to your chosen folder.

The app has no accounts, no cloud backend, no advertising, and no tracking analytics. All health data remains strictly on your local device.
