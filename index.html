Privacy Policy — Temperance Companion

Temperance Companion reads only the Health Connect data types selected by the user and only after the corresponding read permissions are granted: nutrition energy, hydration volume, sleep, steps, distance, active calories burned, total calories burned, exercise, floors climbed, heart rate, resting heart rate, and weight. Reads are foreground, user-triggered operations for the recent seven local days. It never writes to Health Connect and does not request background or extended-history access.

The app stores user-entered habit sessions and notes in an indexed database in private app storage. Existing preference-based history is retained as a migration recovery copy. Android cloud-backup and device-transfer exclusions cover private app data; the app also disables Android backup. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes this local history.

An explicit export writes a seven-day JSON or CSV document named habit-sync-staging.json or habit-sync-staging.csv into any user-selected readable, writable folder. The companion does not create or edit Markdown logs. An explicit recovery-week export reads older locally recorded sessions; it does not request older Health Connect data. The user imports the staging file with the Temperans Habits plugin. Provenance source IDs and session remarks are included in the export. Failed publication can leave previous or pending recovery files in that same folder; users may remove those copies after recovering/importing the desired data. CSV dates use the time zone in plugin Settings.md.

The app has no account system, developer backend, internet permission, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or sale of data. It does not upload data to a developer service. A user-selected document provider or separate vault synchronization application may upload exported files according to that provider's own settings and privacy policy. Exports therefore are not guaranteed to remain exclusively on the phone. Users control the destination and may revoke Health Connect access at any time or remove exported files from that destination.
